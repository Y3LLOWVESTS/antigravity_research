# Research 001 — Schwarzschild-de Sitter

## Objective

Study a known general-relativistic system containing both ordinary central
attraction and an outward contribution associated with a positive
cosmological constant.

## Initial questions

1. What is the exact metric?
2. What weak-field behavior follows from it?
3. What is the static or balance radius?
4. Which effects are coordinate-dependent?
5. What does geodesic deviation show?
6. How large is the effect using the observed cosmological constant?
7. Can analytical and numerical calculations reproduce one another?
