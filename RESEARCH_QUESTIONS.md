# Research Questions

## Central Question

Can a physically consistent configuration produce a measurable gravitational
effect resembling repulsion, defocusing, or reduced gravitational attraction?

## General Relativity

1. Which GR solutions exhibit outward gravitational behavior?
2. Which apparent effects are coordinate artifacts?
3. Which invariant quantities best describe gravitational defocusing?
4. What role does a positive cosmological constant play locally?

## Stress-Energy

1. Which stress-energy tensors can produce defocusing?
2. Which energy conditions would need to be violated?
3. Can pressure contribute to gravitational repulsion?

## Quantum Physics

1. When can quantum fields produce negative local energy density?
2. How strongly do quantum inequalities restrict such effects?
3. Can such effects ever become gravitationally significant?

## Modified Gravity

1. Can viable extensions of GR produce local repulsive behavior?
2. Are such effects compatible with existing observations?

## Computational Research

1. Can we systematically search families of metrics?
2. Can we automatically test physical consistency?
3. Can independent symbolic and numerical methods verify candidates?
