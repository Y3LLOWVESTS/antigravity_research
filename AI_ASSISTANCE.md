# AI Assistance Disclosure

Antigravity Research is a human-directed research project by **Stevan White**.

The project uses **ChatGPT by OpenAI** as an artificial-intelligence research
and development assistant.

## Roles of ChatGPT

ChatGPT may assist with activities including:

- brainstorming research questions;
- mathematical derivations;
- symbolic mathematics;
- numerical-model design;
- Python implementation;
- simulation development;
- test generation;
- debugging;
- dimensional-analysis checks;
- literature-search assistance;
- interpretation of numerical results;
- identifying assumptions and possible errors;
- proposing falsification tests;
- documentation and research-note drafting.

## Human Direction and Verification

Research goals, project direction, decisions about what work to pursue, and
decisions about what material is incorporated into this repository are made
under human direction.

AI-generated material is not automatically treated as scientifically correct.

Important calculations, simulations, interpretations, and claims should be
independently checked through methods such as:

- analytical derivation;
- symbolic verification;
- numerical cross-checking;
- dimensional analysis;
- limiting-case analysis;
- automated tests;
- comparison with established literature;
- independent scientific review where appropriate.

No result should be considered validated merely because it was produced or
reviewed by an AI system.
