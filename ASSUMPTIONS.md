# Assumptions Register

Unless explicitly stated otherwise:

- spacetime is four-dimensional;
- general relativity is the baseline gravitational theory;
- SI units are used for interpreted physical results;
- geometric units may be used when clearly declared;
- coordinate acceleration is not automatically treated as a physical observable;
- invariant or operationally measurable quantities are preferred;
- approximations must be stated explicitly.

Any calculation involving exotic matter, negative energy, modified gravity,
unusual boundary conditions, or extreme extrapolation must identify those
assumptions explicitly.
