## 19. Vacuum, dilute payload and smooth-interface branch — diagnostic, not device shortcut

### 19.1 What empty vacuum does and does not mean

An electric/magnetic field can exist in an evacuated active region with physical sources on or outside its boundaries. In an ideal matter-free region, the matter-trace source of the specific R3 topological loading may vanish locally. A preexisting field gradient may therefore be consistent with the reduced *vacuum* equations.

**But the program requires repulsion on finite neutral matter.** The moment a 1 kg payload enters, its own trace/density gradients, spatial interfaces and stress-energy variation reappear. Evacuating air does not remove the payload's self-backreaction. A physically consistent comparison at fixed 1 kg may redistribute density gradients but cannot simply delete them. Normalize total mass, preserve finite material support and stand-off, include material equation of state and stress, and integrate gradient-layer energy/support. ### 19.4 Hollow payload / double-boundary control

A vacuum cavity can displace material interfaces from the highest-leverage field region, but it also introduces an inner interface. Check both boundaries, source stand-off measured to the **nearest** source material and any hidden internal support. A hollow payload still needs real mass and realistic stress-energy.
