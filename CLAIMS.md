# Claims Register

Every important research result should be classified.

## KNOWN_RESULT

Established result from existing physics or literature.

## REPRODUCED

Known result independently reproduced in this repository.

## NUMERICAL_OBSERVATION

Behavior observed computationally but not yet established analytically.

## CONJECTURE

Possible explanation or model requiring substantially more verification.

## NOVEL_CANDIDATE

Potentially novel result that has survived internal verification but has not
been independently validated.

## REJECTED

Investigated idea found to be incorrect, inconsistent, numerically artificial,
coordinate-dependent, or physically excluded.
